# Product

## Register

product

## Users

MoStar command staff and public-health or government operational teams use the Grid through distinct roles. Command staff needs full Grid, Neo4j, provenance, and governance visibility. Operational teams need scoped regional, health, weather, alert, and reporting views. The interface must never collapse these roles into a shared superuser surface.

## Product Purpose

MoStar Grid is a continuously sensing command surface for Africa. It combines weather, health, infrastructure, memory, and sovereignty observations into truthful continent, region, and country briefings. Success means an operator can see what is known, what is stale, what conflicts, and where coverage is absent without mistaking configured or inferred state for verified reality.

## Brand Personality

Sovereign, vigilant, exact. The product should feel alive and continuously aware without becoming theatrical. Its voice is concise, evidence-led, and operational.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a generic SaaS analytics dashboard, a collection of tiny KPI cards, a political-opinion feed, or a decorative sci-fi cockpit. Avoid fake live states, unqualified confidence, unexplained red counters, cosmetically complete maps, and any interface that hides source disagreement or stale data.

## Design Principles

1. Truth before reassurance: show partial, stale, contested, unavailable, and divergent states explicitly.
2. Provenance at the point of decision: every operational finding exposes source, time, jurisdiction, confidence, and epistemic state.
3. Sense Africa as a system: prioritize continent-wide patterns with region and country drill-downs.
4. Fewer, larger operational surfaces: weather, health, sovereignty, map, briefing, and memory each earn meaningful space.
5. Same contract, adaptive density: command-center desktop carries full context; field/mobile reduces it to maps, alerts, briefings, and actions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Never encode state by color alone. Support keyboard navigation, readable data labels, reduced motion, high-contrast operational states, responsive layouts, and language-ready copy. Optimize first for 1440p and 4K command displays, then for practical field use on mobile connections.
