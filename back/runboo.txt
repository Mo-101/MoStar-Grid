What is wired
-------------
- WooGate is called by semantic_grid.router after TruthEngine and the symbolic Woo interpreter.
- SemanticFrame carries woo_judgment.
- WooTrace persists to JSONL.
- Compatibility config values preserve existing SemanticGrid imports.

Calibration command

The persisted calibration traces JSONL is: woo_calibration_traces.jsonl

Manual comparison caveat
------------------------
woo_20_manual_comparison_not_blind.csv and woo_manual_comparison_summary.json
are provisional only. The comparison was not cold or blind, so it is useful
for disagreement detection, not validation.

Known limitations
-----------------
- No production corpus of actual shipment/signal requests was available.
- The 50 calibration rows are domain-derived realistic inputs.
- The 0.97 threshold has not been validated on production traffic.
- The scorer is not calibrated against historical human judgments.

Engineering recommendation
--------------------------
1. Keep WOO_THRESHOLD at 0.97 for now.
2. Improve feature extraction for evidence/control terms (canary, rollback,
   approval, hold, no-mutation, preservation).
3. Collect real logged inputs and human labels.
4. Either:
   - keep 0.97 as a rare seal threshold and define lower non-seal routing states, or
   - choose a calibrated threshold from labeled production traffic.
